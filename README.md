## 1. Continental Architecture & Repository Naming Standard

All sovereign litigation repositories under the **Global Restitution Alliance** are structured using standardized continental prefixes and ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 sovereign nation codes:
- **`latam-us-<iso2>-<nation>-master-litigation`** (Latin America & Caribbean)
- **`africa-us-<iso2>-<nation>-master-litigation`** (Africa)
- **`asia-us-<iso2>-<nation>-master-litigation`** (Asia & Pacific)
- **`native-us-<state/region>-<nation>-master-litigation`** (Turtle Island Indigenous Nations)

## 3. Cryptographic Verification & Sovereign Trust Commitment

1. **Independent Qualified Settlement Funds (QSF):** All recovery actions mandate court-supervised trust indentures under **26 U.S.C. § 468B** dedicated exclusively to victim annuities, public healthcare, and civilian infrastructure restoration.
2. **Cryptographic Immutability:** Every repository maintains full SHA-256 audit ledgers (`VERIFICATION_KEYS/sha256_manifest.txt`).
3. **Pure Legal Nomenclature:** All filings, pleadings, and briefs strictly enforce formal public international law, admiralty law, and federal civil procedure standards.
